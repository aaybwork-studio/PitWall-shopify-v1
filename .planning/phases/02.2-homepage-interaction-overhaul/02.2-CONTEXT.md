# Phase 02.2: Homepage Interaction Overhaul - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-06-06
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Visiting the homepage (dev server) renders a completely overhauled interactive scrollytelling experience. The page structure is:
1. **Home**: Hero section with video background, tagline, and interactive ticker.
2. **Manifesto**: Background video from the hero keeps playing. One card from each side animates in on scroll to show the tagline.
3. **Featured Product**: Large product layout (left image, right details).
4. **Collections Grid 1**: Horizontal scrolling layout of 9 products and 1 custom title card.
5. **Flipped Featured Product**: Large product layout (left details, right image).
6. **Flipped Collections Grid 2**: Horizontal scrolling layout of 9 products and 1 custom spec card.

**Scrolling Behavior:**
- Desktop: Vertical scrolls translate to horizontal scrolling for Group 1 (Slides 1-4). Once Slide 4 is fully scrolled, vertical scroll snaps down to Slide 5. Scrolling then translates to horizontal scrolling for Group 2 (Slides 5-6). Scrolling past Slide 6 scrolls to the footer.
- Mobile (< 768px): All slides stack vertically in a clean single-column layout with natural vertical scroll.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Scroll-Locking & Translation
- **D-01 (Translation Mechanics):** Translate mouse wheel deltaY and touch swipe gestures to scrollLeft increments on Group 1 and Group 2 containers.
- **D-02 (Boundary Snapping):** When scrolling down reaches the end of Slide 4, unlock vertical scroll and snap-scroll the document viewport to `y = 100vh` (Group 2). When scrolling down reaches the end of Slide 6, unlock vertical scroll to show the footer.
- **D-03 (Reverse Behavior):** When in Group 2 at `scrollLeft = 0` and scrolling up, unlock vertical scroll to snap back up to Group 1.

### Continuous Video Background & Manifesto Cards
- **D-04 (Shared Background):** The autoplaying, looping background video runs continuously behind both Slide 1 (Home) and Slide 2 (Manifesto).
- **D-05 (Card Reveal Animation):** In the Manifesto slide, a Left Card (annotations) and a Right Card (tagline) start off-screen and translate horizontally toward the center proportional to scroll progress between Slide 1 and Slide 2.

### Velocity-Responsive Ticker
- **D-06 (Ticker Scroll Response):** The ticker marquee accelerates or changes direction depending on scroll speed and swipe direction, then smoothly decays back to its default loop speed when scrolling stops.

### Custom Grids & Mirrored Layouts
- **D-07 (Grid 1 Layout):**
  - Col 1: 2 small stacked products.
  - Col 2: 2 small stacked products.
  - Col 3: 1 tall product (top), 1 custom title card (bottom).
  - Col 4: 1 wide product (top), 2 small products side-by-side (bottom).
  - Col 5: 1 tall full-height product.
- **D-08 (Grid 2 Layout):**
  - Col 1: 1 tall product (top), 1 custom spec/text card (bottom).
  - Col 2: 2 small stacked products.
  - Col 3: 2 small stacked products.
  - Col 4: 1 tall full-height product.
  - Col 5: 1 wide product (top), 2 small products side-by-side (bottom).

### Design Aesthetics
- **D-09 (Title Cards):** Title cards will feature high-contrast racing themes (black-on-yellow or yellow borders, monospace telemetry annotations, crosshair symbols, and green pulsing status lights).
- **D-10 (Placeholder Text):** Replace all lorem ipsum and placeholder text with copy that details Pitwall's engineering philosophy, calibration logs, and vehicle specifications.

</decisions>
