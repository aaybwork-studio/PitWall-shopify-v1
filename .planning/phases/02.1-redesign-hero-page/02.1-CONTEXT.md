# Phase 02.1: Redesign Hero Page - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-06-06
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Visiting the homepage (dev server) renders a completely redesigned hero page featuring 4 full-screen sections (Hero, Manifesto, The Collection, About Us) utilizing a strict height-locked (100vh snap-to-section) layout on desktop, falling back to a stacked natural scroll layout on mobile (below 767px). The layout incorporates white cards, rounded card corners (10px), custom layouts per Image 2, a 1/3 screen video player separator element positioned in between the Manifesto and Collection sections, dynamic collection cards that cycle products, and different mode-adaptive background colors.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Section Background Colors
- **D-01 (Adaptive Color Mapping):** Sections alternate between dark and light backgrounds to create visual rhythm. Background colors are bound to CSS variables (`var(--bg)`, `var(--fg)`, `var(--surface)`, `var(--surface-2)`) so they swap seamlessly when dark mode is toggled.
- **D-02 (Section Assignments):**
  - **Section 1 (Hero):** Stays **Dark** in all modes to maintain cinematic contrast.
  - **Section 2 (Manifesto):** **Light** in light mode, **Dark** in dark mode.
  - **Section 3 (The Collection):** **Dark** in light mode, **Light** in dark mode (high contrast).
  - **Section 4 (About Us):** **Light** in light mode, **Dark** in dark mode.

### Dynamic Product Card Rotation
- **D-03 (Auto-Rotation Loop):** Dynamic cards cycle through products on an auto-rotating interval of every 5-7 seconds with a smooth fade transition.
- **D-04 (Hover Overlay Preservation):** On mouse hover, the product rotation pauses and displays the slide-up product details overlay (title, specs, price, hover animation).
- **D-05 (Sourcing):** Products are dynamically pulled from a Shopify Collection (e.g. 'all' collection loop) with a robust hardcoded static fallback array containing at least 6 products for mock rendering if the collection is empty.

### Separator Video Player
- **D-06 (Cinematic Wide Aspect Ratio & Placement):** The 1/3 screen video section is placed between the Manifesto and Collection sections as a separator element (similar to the ticker). It displays a muted, autoplaying, looping video playlist utilizing a wide cinematic aspect ratio (21:9 or 2.39:1).
- **D-07 (Aspect Ratio Scaling on Mobile):** The separator video container dynamically scales on mobile viewports to maintain its 21:9 aspect ratio rather than forcing a 1/3 viewport height, preventing cropping of the video.
- **D-08 (Customizer Sourcing):** Sourced from up to 3 custom video URLs configured via Shopify customizer settings, falling back to preloaded theme assets.

### Card Styling, Spacing & Layout
- **D-09 (Card Sizing & Layout Structure):** Layout structure matches Image 2 exactly:
  - **Hero:** Full-screen video container.
  - **Ticker:** Separates Hero and Manifesto.
  - **Manifesto:** Left column: large Brand Image card. Right column: "the manifesto" title card at the top, and "Place holder text" card below it.
  - **Separator Video:** Full-bleed 1/3 screen video player in between Manifesto and Collection.
  - **The Collection:** 3-column grid containing 5 product cards (4 square cards, 1 tall card spanning 2 rows on the right) and 1 "the collection" title card at the bottom right.
  - **About Us:** Two columns. Left column: "About us" title card + Photo card. Right column: Photo card + Quote card.
- **D-10 (Card Styling):** Cards have a white background, no outer border, borderless spacing (clean gaps/margins between them), and a corner border-radius of **10px** specifically for the hero page cards.
- **D-11 (Padding):** Uniform internal padding is applied inside cards: `24px` on desktop and `16px` on mobile.

### Mobile Responsive Behavior
- **D-12 (Natural Stacked Scrolling):** Strict `100vh` height limits and snap scroll are disabled on mobile viewports (below 767px), allowing all cards to stack vertically in a single column and scroll naturally.
- **D-13 (About Us Mobile Sizing):** The About Us section uses a simplified stack on mobile screens: showing only the "About Us" title card and the Quote card, hiding the two photo cards to keep mobile vertical scroll concise.

### the agent's Discretion
- **D-14:** The agent has discretion over layout alignment, transition details, and default photo assets (using high-quality minimalist photos).

</decisions>
