    ISSUE: Group 2 end still falls to footer instead of snapping to Group 3.
    FIX: The `if (e.deltaY > 0 && curScroll >= maxScroll - 2)` condition inside the `isAtG2` block must call `container.scrollTo({ top: g3Top, behavior: 'smooth' })` and NOT just `return`. Verify Plan 01's change was applied correctly.

    ISSUE: Car position does not update (stuck at -30vw or doesn't move).

    ISSUE: Car moves too fast or jumps.
    FIX: The scroll multiplier `e.deltaY * 0.85` may need tuning. Adjust to 0.7 or 0.6 for a slower, more cinematic pace. Change in both the forward and backward branches of the isAtG3 handler.
