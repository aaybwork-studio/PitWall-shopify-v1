# Phase 02.3 Context — About Us Car Scrollytelling
**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Status:** Ready for planning

---

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Add a new **Group 3** horizontal-then-vertical scrollytelling section to the homepage, inserted between the existing Group 2 (Flipped Grid) and the Footer. The section features an F1 car animating across 3 horizontal panels, then transitions to a vertical "About Us" content section before handing off to the Footer.

**CRITICAL NON-BREAKING CONSTRAINT:** No changes to Group 1 or Group 2 scroll logic, refs, or state. Group 3 is appended after Group 2's closing div, before `<Footer />`. The existing wheel-intercept handler must be extended to handle the Group 3 zone — existing Group 1/2 boundaries stay untouched.

**Full scroll sequence after this phase:**
1. Hero (video bg, ticker) — Group 1 horizontal
2. Manifesto cards — Group 1 horizontal
3. Featured Product — Group 1 horizontal
4. Collections Grid 1 — Group 1 horizontal
5. Flipped Featured Product — Group 2 horizontal
6. Flipped Collections Grid 2 — Group 2 horizontal
7. **[NEW] About Us — Panel 1: Entry** — Group 3 horizontal
8. **[NEW] About Us — Panel 2: Race track separator** — Group 3 horizontal
9. **[NEW] About Us — Panel 3: Founding story + car stop** — Group 3 horizontal
10. **[NEW] Vertical content section** — normal vertical scroll
11. Footer
</domain>

<decisions>

## Car Asset
- **D-01 (Car):** Use a custom no-branding placeholder F1 car — no McLaren/Ferrari logos or livery colors. Generate as an SVG or CSS-drawn silhouette for now. The car should be a clean side-profile F1 shape (same visual register as the ESPN McLaren reference). A future phase will replace with a real branded asset.
- **D-02 (Car direction):** Car enters from the left edge of Panel 1, travels rightward across Panel 2, and stops at the left edge of the text block in Panel 3.
- **D-03 (Car animation mechanic):** Car `translateX` is driven by the Group 3 `scrollLeft` value — same scroll-position-to-transform pattern as the ESPN reference. The car moves proportionally to scroll progress across all 3 panels. No GSAP — use `transform: translateX()` driven by scroll listener, same as existing ticker offset pattern.

## Horizontal Scroll Panels (Group 3 — 3 panels)

- **D-04 (Panel 1 — About Us Entry):** Full `100vw` × `100vh` slide. Large "ABOUT US" text fills the background in a massive display font (same `font-display-strict` variable used elsewhere). Text is extremely large, low-opacity, acting as a texture/wallpaper behind the car. The car enters from off-screen left. Aesthetic: ESPN-style, the typography IS the background.
- **D-05 (Panel 2 — Race Track Separator):** Full `100vw` × `100vh` slide. Race-track inspired visual: horizontal track lines, grid-start box graphic, or telemetry chart lines rendered in CSS/SVG. Minimal, monochromatic (white/gold on dark). The car races across this panel at full speed (linear progression). No heavy copy — this panel is a cinematic beat between entry and destination.
- **D-06 (Panel 3 — Founding Story / Car Stop):** Full `100vw` × `100vh` slide. The car decelerates and stops at the left-center of the panel. Right side shows: a short founding story paragraph (2–3 sentences), and an "OUR STORY →" CTA button linking to `/pages/about`. Typography follows existing mono/display-strict hierarchy. The car's final resting position is flush with the left of the text column.

## Vertical Section (after horizontal scroll exits)

- **D-07 (Transition):** When the user scrolls past Panel 3's right edge, the page unlocks vertically and scrolls down into the vertical content section — same snap behavior as Group 1→Group 2 transition.
- **D-08 (Stats row):** 3 typographic stat columns in large display numerals. Stats are brand-philosophy statements derived from existing site slogans, NOT team/season counts. Use the existing "and life has always been a race." and "PITWALL ENGINEERING / SPEED CALIBRATION" voice. Example placeholders: `"ZERO / COMPROMISE"` / `"ONE / PURSUIT"` / `"INFINITE / PRECISION"` — each with a mono label beneath. Real copy TBD by client.
- **D-09 (Brand paragraph):** Short paragraph in body font, mid-opacity, about what Pitwall represents. Placeholder copy derived from existing manifesto tone.
- **D-10 (CTA):** `"EXPLORE OUR STORY →"` button, same style as existing Add to Collection buttons (border, mono font, uppercase, `WM.text` color, hover: opacity 0.7). Links to `/pages/about`.

## Design Aesthetics

- **D-11 (Background):** `WM.bg` (`#0F0C09`) for horizontal panels — matches the rest of the homepage dark aesthetic exactly.
- **D-12 (Car color):** Neutral dark-charcoal or white silhouette — no brand colors on the placeholder car.
- **D-13 (No new tokens):** All colors via existing `WM.*` object or CSS vars. No raw hex values.
- **D-14 (Gold accents):** `WM.gold` (`#E8B93B`) for panel labels, stat labels, and CTA hover — same as rest of page.

## Mobile Behavior

- **D-15 (Mobile collapse):** Below 768px, Group 3 collapses to natural vertical scroll — same pattern as Group 1 and Group 2. The 3 panels stack vertically. The car is shown as a static image (no scroll-driven animation). The vertical content section renders normally.

## Shopify Integration

- **D-16 (Schema-driven):** All user-facing text is editable via Shopify theme customizer. Schema settings required:
  - `about_heading` — Panel 1 background text (default: "ABOUT US")
  - `founding_story` — Panel 3 short paragraph (textarea)
  - `cta_label` — CTA button text (default: "OUR STORY")
  - `cta_url` — CTA button URL (default: `/pages/about`)
  - `stat_1_value`, `stat_1_label` × 3 — the 3 stat columns
  - `brand_paragraph` — vertical section body paragraph
- **D-17 (Mount point):** The car section is part of the existing `HomepageScrollytelling` React component — not a new Liquid section. Schema values are passed via the existing `data-*` attribute pattern on `#homepage-interactive-root` (or equivalent mount point in `hero-canvas.liquid`).

</decisions>
