# Phase 02.4 Discussion Log — Redesign Collections Page
**Date:** 2026-06-07

---

## Alignments & Decisions Mapped

### 1. Card Styles & Vertical Scrolling
- **Alignment:** The Collections page will use cards styled identically to the homepage/landing page cards, complete with specification labels and hover details overlay.
- **Scroll Behavior:** Scrolling is normal vertical scroll with NO scroll snap.
- **CTA on hover:** The hover details overlay will show a "VIEW" CTA button that navigates directly to the product URL, rather than "CALIBRATE".

### 2. Yellow Filter/Sort Bar
- **Categories:** The categories filter dropdown will support the full finalised product categories list (All, WLED Light Boxes, Car Models, 2D Wall Art, Keychains, Desk Accessories, Layered Art, Driver Figurines), pre-selected to "All".
- **Filters/Sort Layout:** The yellow bar layout will feature Categories on the left and Sort by on the right. Both dropdown buttons will render the currently active option name next to them (e.g. "Sort by: Price: Low to High").
- **Dropdown Style:** The submenus will open as smaller, brutalist dropdown panels overlaying the product grid.

### 3. Product Cards Grid Layout
- **Aspect Ratios:** Random aspect ratios (Square, Wide, Tall) will be assigned to products in a masonry grid. The alignment must stay locked to the grid layout.
- **Initial Load:** Shuffles all products into random order on initial page load.

### 4. Video Hero Header
- **Top Video:** Reuses the video playlist asset currently active in the homepage hero (`F1_car_idling_on_background_202606011707.mp4`). It will occupy 1/3 of the screen height.
- **Title Overlay:** Center-aligned "THE COLLECTION" title overlaying the video with `mix-blend-mode: difference` for high-contrast visibility.
