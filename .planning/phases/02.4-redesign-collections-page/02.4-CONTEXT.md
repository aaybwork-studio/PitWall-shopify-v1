# Phase 02.4 Context — Redesign Collections Page
**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Status:** Ready for planning

---

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Redesign the Collections page (`/collections/all`) to feature a video hero header, a yellow filter/sort bar, and a dynamic masonry-style product grid matching the landing page aesthetic. The entire page below the header/footer will be driven by a React component mounted in `sections/collection-all.liquid`.

**Full layout sequence of the collections page:**
1. Global Navigation Header (from layout/theme.liquid)
2. Hero Video Banner (1/3 screen height) with blended "THE COLLECTION" title
3. Yellow Filter/Sort Bar (Category on left, Sort on right, with dropdown menus)
4. Dynamic Masonry Product Grid (random order, random positions/sizes, no scroll snap)
5. Global Footer (from layout/theme.liquid)
</domain>

---

<decisions>

## Top Video Banner
- **D-01 (Video):** Reuse the video playlist background asset from the home page scrollytelling (`F1_car_idling_on_background_202606011707.mp4`). It should occupy 1/3 of the screen height.
- **D-02 (Title overlay):** Display the text "THE COLLECTION" centered over the video in the large display font. Use `mix-blend-mode: difference` for high-contrast blending with the moving video behind it.

## Yellow Filter/Sort Bar
- **D-03 (Background):** Bright racing yellow background (`#F6C917` / `#E8B93B` or matching `WM.gold`).
- **D-04 (Categories):** Left-aligned dropdown button with the currently selected category displayed next to it (e.g., "Category: WLED Light Boxes"). Pre-selected default is "All".
- **D-05 (Categories list):**
  - All
  - WLED Light Boxes
  - Car Models
  - 2D Wall Art
  - Keychains
  - Desk Accessories
  - Layered Art
  - Driver Figurines
- **D-06 (Sorting):** Right-aligned dropdown button with the currently selected sort option displayed next to it. Options include:
  - Default (random order)
  - Price: Low to High
  - Price: High to Low
  - Alphabetical
- **D-07 (Dropdown style):** Brutalist, smaller dropdown panels overlaying the grid when toggled.

## Product Grid & Cards
- **D-08 (No scroll snap):** Only natural vertical scrolling.
- **D-09 (Grid Layout):** A responsive grid where products have randomized aspect ratios (Square, Wide, Tall) but remain perfectly aligned to grid lines.
- **D-10 (Random order):** Products are shuffled into random order on initial load.
- **D-11 (Landing Page Card Style):** Cards reuse the design from the homepage dashboard cards (full-bleed product image background, specification pre-order badge, minimal footer, hover specifications overlay).
- **D-12 (Hover behavior):** Slide-up details overlay on hover. No "CALIBRATE" button. Instead, display a "VIEW" CTA button that navigates directly to the product URL.
- **D-13 (Products Data):** Populate the products using the finalised F1 product list.

</decisions>
